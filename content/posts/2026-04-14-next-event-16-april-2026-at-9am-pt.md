Arnaud is a solution architect and creative technologist from Moment Factory Pipeline Team, leading R&D for disruptive workflows across digital content production and experiential venue spaces. [Moment Factory](https://momentfactory.com/) is a world-renowned multidisciplinary entertainment studio specializing in the creation, design, and production of experiential environments. Blurring the lines between the real, the imagined, and the digital allows Moment Factory to unlock new ways to tell stories, engage the senses, and elicit strong, lasting emotions.
