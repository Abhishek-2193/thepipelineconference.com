## June 2026

**Thursday, June 4, 2026 from 3pm - 4:30pm Pacific Time  
[The Future of Experiential Services](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/the-future-of-experiential-services-tickets-1990060281874?aff=TPCWebsite)**  
**Video** will be recorded and uploaded to [The Pipeline Conference YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ThePipelineConference/featured)

**Speaker**

- **Phil Galler, Founder & CTO at Seismiq & 180 Studios**

**Executive Host**

- **Lionel Ohayon, Chief Creative Officer at Journey Studio**

**Bios**

**Phil Galler**  
Phil Galler is a pioneering creative technologist and entrepreneur renowned for his transformative impact on the film, television, and live event industries. Most notably, he co-founded Lux Machina, where he became a central figure in the development of Virtual Production and famously coined the term ICVFX (In-Camera Visual Effects). His technical leadership was instrumental in the success of landmark projects such as The Mandalorian, Rogue One, and House of the Dragon, as well as major live spectacles like the League of Legends World Championships. Beyond his production accolades, Galler is recognized as a strategic innovator who bridges the gap between complex software engineering and high-end creative storytelling, often collaborating with institutions like the USC Entertainment Technology Center and organizations like the DGA to define the future of intelligent creation and AI-integrated filmmaking.

![](/uploads/2026/05/image-1024x649.png)

**Lionel Ohayon – Chief Creative Officer at Journey Studio**  
Lionel Ohayon is the Co-Founder and Chief Creative Officer of Journey and the Founder of ICRAVE, a world-renowned innovation and design studio. With over two decades of experience, he has transitioned from reinventing New York’s Meatpacking District and award-winning airport terminals to pioneering the "Physical-to-Digital" (P2D) landscape. Most recently, his work has focused on the intersection of architecture and emerging technology, highlighted by his pivotal role in the experiential design of the Las Vegas Sphere and the development of immersive ecosystems for healthcare and sports wellness. Now based in Miami, Ohayon leads Journey’s creative vision by integrating AI, digital twins, and design thinking to solve complex social and business challenges, ensuring that the next generation of physical environments is seamlessly connected to the virtual world.

![](/uploads/2026/05/image-1-1024x1024.png)

**Executive Host**

**Bios**

********Jonathan Gagnon********  
Jonathan Gagnon is a prominent figure in the visual effects (VFX) industry, currently serving as the Director of Research & Development (R&D) at FOLKS VFX, which is part of the global Pitch Black Company group.

Jonathan has a deep technical background in computer graphics and pipeline development. Before his current leadership role, he served as a Senior Pipeline Technical Director. He is well-known in the industry for his expertise in VFX workflows and has been a featured speaker at major events such as The Pipeline Conference (TPC) and CAFÉ VFX, where he discusses R&D strategies and pipeline modernization.

He holds a PhD in Computer Science (specializing in Computer Graphics) from the École de Technologie Supérieure (ÉTS) in Montréal. His research has focused on complex areas like fluid simulation surfaces, texturing, and snow simulation.

![](/uploads/2026/04/image-1.png)

************Andrew Lazarow************  
Andrew Lazarow is a Studio Leader and Director at LAB at Rockwell Group, the firm’s specialized studio dedicated to experience design and the integration of emerging technology within the built environment.

With a background rooted in theatrical storytelling and interactive telecommunications, Andrew leads a multidisciplinary team to create immersive environments that bridge the gap between digital innovation and physical space. His work focuses on finding the “emotional core” of a narrative and leveraging technologies—such as motion capture, projection mapping, and interactive lighting—to transform branded environments, corporate headquarters, and public spaces into memorable experiences.

AB at Rockwell Group is the award-winning experience design and technology studio embedded within the world-renowned architecture and design firm, Rockwell Group. Founded by David Rockwell to push the boundaries of physical space, the LAB functions as a creative laboratory where architects, creative coders, interaction designers, and strategists collaborate to merge the digital and physical worlds.

The LAB specializes in a multidisciplinary approach that spans the entire project lifecycle, from initial strategy to technical implementation:  
– Experience Strategy & Narrative: Defining the “emotional core” of a space and how users journey through it.  
– Interactive Installations: Designing systems that react to human movement, touch, or environmental data.  
– Digital Placemaking: Using large-scale media, light, and sound to define the identity of urban spaces and corporate headquarters.  
– Virtual & Hybrid Environments: Creating digital twins and immersive platforms that extend physical experiences into the virtual realm.  
– Content Creation: Producing custom cinematic and generative media tailored to unique architectural canvases.

**Bios**

******Arnaud Grosjean******  
Arnaud Grosjean is a Solution Architect and Creative Technologist specialized in the convergence of multimedia, IT, and interactive system design. As a key member of Moment Factory’s Pipeline Team, he leads the R&D of disruptive workflows that bridge the gap between digital content creation and physical venues.

Arnaud’s work focuses on future-proofing immersive infrastructure. He actively explores the potential of "Digital Twins" and simulation technologies to virtually commission complex multimedia systems—testing lighting, media, and interactivity before physical deployment.

A vocal advocate for open standards in the Location-Based Entertainment (LBE) sector, Arnaud promotes interoperability through open protocols and formats. His expertise in blending AV architectures with IT backbones has been pivotal in major technical refreshes and large-scale activations, including the IP-based overhaul of the iconic Cipriani 25 Broadway in New York, the AT&T Discovery District, and the Hong Kong International Airport.

![](/uploads/2026/03/image-950x1024.jpeg)

******Marc Petit******  
Marc Petit is a veteran leader in computer graphics and real‑time 3D technology with over four decades of experience spanning software development, media & entertainment, and immersive digital ecosystems. He is best known for his tenure as Vice President and General Manager of Unreal Engine at Epic Games, where he oversaw the platform’s expansion across industries, including gaming, film and television, architecture, engineering, automotive, simulation, and manufacturing.

Before joining Epic Games in 2016, Petit served as a partner and Entrepreneur‑in‑Residence at Montreal’s XPND Capital, where he co‑founded Taxelco, an innovative, sustainable urban transportation venture. He previously led Autodesk’s Media & Entertainment division from 2002 to 2013, directing the development and global market strategy for major 3D and VFX tools such as 3ds Max, Maya, MotionBuilder, and Flame.

Most recently, he has shifted focus toward large‑scale immersive experiences and strategic advisory roles, including consulting with Moment Factory on real‑world interactive media installations. His career reflects a consistent focus on pioneering real‑time content creation, innovation in digital storytelling, and the fusion of technology with creative industries.

**Eric W. Shamlin**  
Eric Shamlin is a highly accomplished creative executive and producer operating at the intersection of traditional storytelling and emerging technology. Eric is currently CEO of Firebringer. Eric previously held leadership positions at a number of modern studios, including CEO of Secret Level, an AI-native entertainment studio that focuses on reimagining how stories are developed and produced across film, television, and advertising. With a career spanning three decades, Shamlin has held senior leadership roles at major organizations including Media.Monks, where he served as EVP Global Head of Entertainment, as well as positions at Apple and Omnicom. His work has been recognized with some of the industry's most prestigious honors, including multiple Emmy Awards, Peabody Awards, and over a dozen Cannes Lions.

In addition to his studio leadership, Shamlin is a prominent figure in the professional creative community and a leading voice on the ethical integration of AI in media. He currently serves on the Board of Governors for the Television Academy (the Emmys), where he chairs the Innovation Standing Committee and the AI Task Force. He also holds the role of Innovation Chair for the Producers Guild of America and serves as an advisor for the SXSW film festival. An educator and frequent keynote speaker, he has lectured at institutions like UCLA and USC, sharing his expertise on the evolution of immersive media and the "democratization" of production through new technological tools.
