Pipeline developers are often exclusively focused on improving the workflow for artists and production. Given that, their development workflow often succumbs to inefficiencies and neglect over the long-term. Many studios realize the value of spending effort on DevOps, not only for the benefit of software development teams but also for the artists and production staff downstream. This talk will take a look at some of the DevOps practices at a few studios and the ROI they provide.
