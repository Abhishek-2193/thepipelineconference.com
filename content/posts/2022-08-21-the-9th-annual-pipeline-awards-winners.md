## Pipeline Tool Award Winner - OCIO

![logo, company name](https://media.licdn.com/dms/image/C4E22AQGYo_ArMhFL0g/feedshare-shrink_2048_1536/0/1660237686728?e=1695254400&v=beta&t=D7OXjT888XO1WyOVORtOUrCbvP8tfHH4fu_Mz4BfL94)

OpenColorIO (OCIO) is the complete color management solution geared towards motion picture production with an emphasis on visual effects and computer animation.

As such, OCIO helps enforce a color management methodology that is required for high fidelity color imaging in modern computer graphics. Version 1 was released in 2011 by [Jeremy Selan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAABzUuQBq02z_xf04eKuzqgeuOxklomOGnw) at [Sony Pictures Imageworks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sony-pictures-imageworks/).

OCIO became the way to implement ACES at many studios and across multiple DCCs. In 2017 development on version 2 started, with an OCIO V2 working group under [Academy Software Foundation](https://www.linkedin.com/company/academysoftwarefoundation/) were developers and color scientists from across the industry who hammered out the framework for what is now a very robust industry standard for managing color through the production pipeline.

This award was presented to ASWF's Open Color IO core contributors, [Carol Payne](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAA6Yp38BXxX85mA-UnB_1J1nVLqUZp1qJUs), [Michael Dolan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAKeX5sBOITXKfCwdLddqYYqDgcLrxJUIZg), [Patrick Hodoul](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAABoLFABBVUZ9TIhzTPr1u0UZwdf763fkXc), [Thomas Mansencal](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAYOyWwB64oanD51W-dfO3t6uqd5eNDWt6I), and Doug Walker.

Congratulations to OCIO community 🎉

Check out OCIO's official site: <https://opencolorio.org/>

## Pipeline Hero Award Winner - Tram Le-Jones

![No alternative text description for this image](https://media.licdn.com/dms/image/C5622AQGgQVr7Evu00w/feedshare-shrink_2048_1536/0/1660180598679?e=1695254400&v=beta&t=xmXIl-4Hkw-kz4cGADdai7Ma6YuuDhchUjgEVOkidkE)

Tram is currently the VP of Solutions for [ftrack](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ftrack/), a mother of twins, wife and a tireless organizer for The Pipeline Conference as well as the L.A. Pipeline Meetup.

An industry veteran, starting out at ESC Entertainment in 2003 and working at such studios as Rhythm & Hues, Digital Domain, Sony Pictures Imageworks, Method and more. It was at Framestore where she co-founded the L.A. Pipeline Meetup in 2013. TPC was a natural extension of the Pipeline Meetup.

Her energy, knowledge and understanding along with a great personality guide these organizations, along with [Fran Zandonella](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAADLazQBs5gKt4ivSQgIJImOto1mxPcw5kw) and Alexis Casas, Tram has been instrumental in helping gather the pipeline community together to share ideas and learn from one another.

Congratulations, Tram!
