The Pipeline Conference is thrilled to announce that the nominations for the 9th Annual Pipeline Awards are now open. The 9th Annual Pipeline Awards are being held on Sunday, August 7 2022 in Vancouver, BC in collaboration with DigiPro 2022.

> The Pipeline Awards recognizes and honors the outstanding tools used in production and the individuals that further CG pipeline technologies.

Know of anyone that fits into the following award categories? If so, please go ahead and click on the appropriate link to nominate.

**Pipeline Tool Award**  
Honors the tools worthy of industry recognition for the benefits they have brought to one or more studios.

[Nominate a tool](https://forms.gle/GuwJSb4dFKTQURTw9)

**Pipeline Hero Award**  
Honors somebody who stands out in the industry by contributing to pipeline technologies at a studio or in general.

[Nominate individuals](https://forms.gle/GuwJSb4dFKTQURTw9)

You can find more information and the history of the awards at [/pipeline-awards/.](/pipeline-awards/)
