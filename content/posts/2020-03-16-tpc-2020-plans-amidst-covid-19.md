Hello Pipeline Folks,  
  
By now, you have probably received many notices from many organizations about COVID-19 and various events closing.  You may be wondering, how will this affect The Pipeline Conference in 2020?  
  
 **We are still planning to hold our event on 19 July 2020 either in person or virtually.** Our plan is to follow SIGGRAPH’s lead. If SIGGRAPH hosts the conference in Washington D.C., we will host TPC2020 there as well. If SIGGRAPH does not happen in D.C., we will figure out what hosting TPC2020 virtually looks like.   
  
 Even with the uncertainty, we believe in the community of pipeline developers.  We want to celebrate your accomplishments throughout the year and provide a forum for learning and sharing.   
  
We still encourage everybody to **[submit proposals for talks](/cfp-tpc2020-talks-and-panels/)** or nominations for the **[pipeline awards](/cfp-tpc2020-pipeline-awards/).**

We will keep you posted as we learn more.  
  
Sincerely,  
The TPC 2020 Team
