We're doing it again! The Pipeline Conference is bringing you another full day of pipeline-related "Birds of a Feather" sessions at SIGGRAPH in Vancouver, BC, Canada. Our sessions will be held on Tuesday, August 12, 2025 starting at 9am in the [East Building, Room 13](https://s2025.conference-schedule.org/?post_type=page&p=20&location=rEast-Building-Room-13).   
**NOTE**: Attendees must [register](https://s2025.siggraph.org/register/) for a SIGGRAPH Experience or Full Conference pass to attend.  
These sessions are ***in person*** and ***will not be recorded***. Photos and notes will be taken.

We will be ending the day with our traditional "Beers of a Feather" event sponsored by AWS.

To get an invite to our "Beers of a Feather" evening event, you must attend at least one our "Birds of a Feather" sessions.  
****NOTE:*******Entry to the event is first come, first served*** if you are ****registered**** for our event. Our event space is limited and an RSVP does not guarantee entry to this event.

We hope to see you at SIGGRAPH this year!
