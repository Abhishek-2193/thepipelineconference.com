AWS Thinkbox helps creative studios and visual artists harness the power of the cloud by providing a conduit to AWS and near-limitless compute capacity for rendering, virtual workstations, and storage. Acquired in March 2017, AWS Thinkbox develops Deadline, a high-volume compute management software application that helps studios optimize and scale their compute resources on premises or on the cloud. AWS Thinkbox products are widely used across entertainment, design, architecture, and engineering.
