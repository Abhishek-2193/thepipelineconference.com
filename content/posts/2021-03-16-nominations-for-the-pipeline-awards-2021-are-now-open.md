The Pipeline Conference is thrilled to announce that the nominations for The Pipeline Awards 2021 are now open.

> The Pipeline Awards recognizes and honors the outstanding tools used in production and the individuals that further CG pipeline technologies.

Know of anyone that fits into the following award categories? If so, please go ahead and click on the appropriate link to nominate.

**Pipeline Tool Award**  
This award honors the tools worthy of industry recognition for the benefits they have brought to one or more studios.

**Pipeline Hero Award**  
This award honors people who stand out in the industry contributing to pipeline technologies at a studio or in general.
