Did you know that TPC (The Pipeline Conference) was inspired by discussions at BOF (Birds of a Feather) sessions that have been taking place at SIGGRAPH since 2010?

What is a Birds of a Feather (BOF)?

*Informal presentations, discussions, and demonstrations for people who share interests, goals, technologies, environments, or backgrounds.*

If you are are a beginner, practitioner or just curious about pipelines that are used in Media and Entertainment industries, then please come join us at SIGGRAPH 2020 BOF.

> [Global VFX Pipelines](https://s2020.siggraph.org/presentation/?id=bof_136&sess=sess503) - 08/27/2020 12:00 - 13:00 PDT
>
>   
> An informal gathering for anyone interested in hearing about and discussing the latest trends in production pipelines.  
>   
> Contributors:  
> Alex Schworer, Netflix  
> Rob Blau, Autodesk  
> Fran Zandonella Benjamin, ZOIC STUDIOS; Digital Pipeline Developers, Inc

NOTE: In past years, we've been able to let anyone in for free (though technically we are not supposed to do that).  This year, I don't think we have much choice that everyone needs to be attending SIGGRAPH to join us at the Pipeline BOF.

We hope to see you on Thursday, 27th August at 12:00 pm.

Thank you.
