The Pipeline Awards have joined The Pipeline Conference, making the 7th year bigger than ever and open for all. The Pipeline Awards honor the outstanding tools used in production and the individuals that further pipeline technologies for studios of all sizes.   
  
We are happy to announce The Pipeline Conference will be taking over ownership of [Shotgun’s Pipeline Awards](https://www.shotgunsoftware.com/blog/congrats-to-this-years-pipeline-award-winners/), helping to celebrate the best work done in our community throughout the year. We hope to see the awards grow into a general industry celebration, shining a spotlight into this important area of production which can often go unnoticed.

We are now accepting entries for these awards. Please [send us your nominations](https://forms.gle/yWHoCCB4h9xauRJx9) by **Friday, June 5th, 2020** for consideration.  
  
Nominations should be in one of these two categories:

- **Tools Award**: Awards given to tools worthy of industry recognition for the benefits they’ve brought to one or more studios.
- **Hero Award**: Award given to somebody who stands out in the industry, who improves not only their own studio, but contributes to pipeline technologies in general.

[Submit entry for The Pipeline Awards](https://forms.gle/yWHoCCB4h9xauRJx9)

Winners will be announced at this year’s TPC, on Sunday, July 19, 2020.
