> We have been slowly moving towards more and more people working remotely, but one of the legacies of COVID and lockdown was that it pushed us more rapidly into remote working which had for a long time thought to be impossible by studio heads, if we wanted to be able to protect their IP.
>
> How have things changed? In just one year we've been hearing about many different offerings for remote working. What are the differences between systems and what can we expect in the future?
>
