TPC 2026 is running online. Our in-person conference in Vancouver was cancelled; the programme built for it is being delivered as two free half-day events from October, followed by the tenth Pipeline Awards in December.

Sessions are recorded where permission is given and published on [The Pipeline Conference YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ThePipelineConference).

## The 2026 online programme

### The Agentic Pipeline — half day one

**October 2026 · date to be announced**  
**Online · Free to attend · Speakers to be announced**

AI and agentic workflows in digital media pipelines: agentic workflows for asset production, the data layers and security model underneath, DevOps and FinOps for AI services, and honest accounts of what broke along the way.

### Operational Best Practices — half day two

**Date to be announced**  
**Online · Free to attend · Speakers to be announced**

Pipeline engineering and operations at production scale: render farms, storage, OpenUSD in anger, cloud adoption, long migrations, and keeping the whole thing standing while a show is in flight. A day of classic Pipeline Conference content on practical topics that matter, kept apart from the chaos and bustle of new workflows introduced by AI.

## June 2026

**Thursday, June 4, 2026 from 3pm - 4:30pm Pacific Time  
The Future of Experiential Services**  
**[Register here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/the-future-of-experiential-services-tickets-1990060281874?aff=TPCWebsite)**  
via Zoom  
**Video** will be recorded and put onto the [The Pipeline Conference youtube channel](https://www.youtube.com/c/ThePipelineConference)

In the next generation of hospitality experiences, the venue is no longer a backdrop—it is a dynamic participant in the guest journey.

In the next generation of hospitality experiences, the venue is no longer a backdrop—it is a dynamic participant in the guest journey. We are transitioning from the era of static environments into a new age of 'Shared Experiential Reality.' This session explores how the convergence of curved 16K LED architecture and 3D audio beamforming allows leaders to curate communal, high-sensory experiences that were once physically impossible.

We will examine the 'Responsive Venue'—a space that leverages real-time services to personalize the journey for audiences, delivering story-driven narratives to individual seats and breathing life into architectural surfaces with digital light. By analyzing the technology blueprints of icons like the Sphere, Disney, and COSM, we provide a strategic roadmap for design groups to move beyond traditional entertainment into software-defined environments that command audience obsession and redefine brand loyalty.

**Speakers**  
**Phil Galler – Founder & CTO at Seismiq & 180 Studios**  
Phil Galler is a pioneering creative technologist and entrepreneur renowned for his transformative impact on the film, television, and live event industries. Most notably, he co-founded Lux Machina, where he became a central figure in the development of Virtual Production and famously coined the term ICVFX (In-Camera Visual Effects). His technical leadership was instrumental in the success of landmark projects such as The Mandalorian, Rogue One, and House of the Dragon, as well as major live spectacles like the League of Legends World Championships. Beyond his production accolades, Galler is recognized as a strategic innovator who bridges the gap between complex software engineering and high-end creative storytelling, often collaborating with institutions like the USC Entertainment Technology Center and organizations like the DGA to define the future of intelligent creation and AI-integrated filmmaking.

**Executive Host**  
**Lionel Ohayon – Chief Creative Officer at Journey Studio**  
Lionel Ohayon is the Co-Founder and Chief Creative Officer of Journey and the Founder of ICRAVE, a world-renowned innovation and design studio. With over two decades of experience, he has transitioned from reinventing New York’s Meatpacking District and award-winning airport terminals to pioneering the "Physical-to-Digital" (P2D) landscape. Most recently, his work has focused on the intersection of architecture and emerging technology, highlighted by his pivotal role in the experiential design of the Las Vegas Sphere and the development of immersive ecosystems for healthcare and sports wellness. Now based in Miami, Ohayon leads Journey’s creative vision by integrating AI, digital twins, and design thinking to solve complex social and business challenges, ensuring that the next generation of physical environments is seamlessly connected to the virtual world.
