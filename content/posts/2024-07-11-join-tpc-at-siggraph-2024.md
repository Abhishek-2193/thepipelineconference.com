The Pipeline Conference is joining forces with our fellow pipeline community organizers at Siggraph this year to host a full day of pipeline-related "Birds of a Feather" sessions on Tuesday, July 30, 2024. We will be ending the day with a Beers of a Feather event that has been generously sponsored by CoreWeave!

You'll need to attend at least one of our "Birds of a Feather" sessions to get an invite to the "*Beers* of a Feather" event later in the evening - we hope to see you at Siggraph this year!
