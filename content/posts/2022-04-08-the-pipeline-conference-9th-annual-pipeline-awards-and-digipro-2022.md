*We’re excited to announce the continued collaboration between [The Pipeline Conference](/) and [DigiPro](http://digiproconf.org/). We are joining forces this year to bring you a hybrid conference experience.*

> [DigiPro 2022](https://digiproconf.org/) and the [9th Annual Pipeline Awards](/pipeline-awards/) will be held in Vancouver, BC on Sunday 7 August 2022.

> The Pipeline Conference will be held virtually on Tuesday 23 August 2022. DigiPro 2022 recorded content will also be available virtually on this day.

#### Call for Submissions

[The Pipeline Awards](/pipeline-awards/) (Deadline is midnight Friday, July 1, 2022)

[The Pipeline Conference](/2022/04/08/tpc-2022-call-for-participation/) (Deadline is midnight Friday, July 1, 2022)

Details about DigiPro submission are [available at this link](https://digiproconf.org/submit/).

We look forward to your submissions and another memorable conference experience this year.
