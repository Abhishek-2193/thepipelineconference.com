Hello Pipeline Folks,

The Pipeline Conference is going fully virtual.  With this change, we are considering changing the date of the conference so more people can attend and participate.  UPDATED: We expect to decide by 16th of July 2020 and will keep you informed.

Registration information should be coming near the end of May.  With the conference going virtual, we are intending to make this a low-cost or free event (depends on our sponsors!).  Subscribe to our newsletter here:  [The Pipeline Conference Newsletter](https://thepipelineconference.us20.list-manage.com/subscribe/post?u=dfa8d1ff61cbcc87feb04ac10&id=29616c2928) and keep an eye on our [website](https://www.thepipelineconference.com).

We are still seeking sponsors.  If you or your company are interested in sponsoring, please contact [sponsors@thepipelineconference.com](mailto:sponsors@thepipelineconference.com).

We believe in our community of pipeline developers.  We want to celebrate your accomplishments, and provide an environment for learning and sharing.

We encourage everybody to submit proposals for [talks](/cfp-tpc2020-talks-and-panels/) or nominations for the [pipeline awards](/cfp-tpc2020-pipeline-awards/) by Friday, June 5, 2020:

<https://easychair.org/cfp/TPC2020>

Sincerely,

The TPC 2020 Team
